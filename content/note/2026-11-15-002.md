> In an essay for ARTnews, writer Kyle Chayka notes that most titles for people who make things—painter, DJ, choreographer—“signify a particular craft or practice, a discipline that takes years to develop, an identity that one doesn’t just become overnight." Meanwhile, the modern “creator” is “generic [and] commodified… defined by the ability to monetize online.”
>  
> -- [audience of one](https://open.substack.com/pub/jasmine/p/audience-of-one?r=7cjysr&utm_campaign=post&utm_medium=web)

This rings very true. It seems pretty bad to generalise the _craftsman_ to an all-encompassing, income-driven _creator_.
