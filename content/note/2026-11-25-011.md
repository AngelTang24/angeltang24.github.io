Am I the only one [who would prefer Violet's resume](https://practicaltypography.com/why-does-typography-matter.html)? Her layout doesn't seem any less formal, nor is the overall appearance less readable. Sure, Trixie employs more modern looking fonts and admittedly that Serif one is quite appealing, but then it also looks like something from a Canva template. I'm a bit tired of Canva and the "modern, sleek" layout of websites nowadays. So I would go for Violet's.

Also, he analogises good typography as dressing properly at a job interview. I don't think the analogy is very apt, as there probably isn't too much a need to pay meticulous detail to your dressing, as long as it's suitably done. But still I somewhat-ish get the merits of good typography. 

For me I am my only reader (sadly), so I'm doing this out of interest. So reasons don't really matter to me, but these are just things I thought to point out.

That's fair. But here I was specifically told to make a 2s decision based off typography, so I don't think that counts. I guess for (1) you could say it would be safer to appeal to a majority preference, i.e., Trixie's resume, but here that decision is based off that one admissions/HR guy whose interests could go both ways. But I'm nitpicking here.
