i’m really really gratified by the way things’ve turned out. i’m not exaggerating, or running another “from-my-humble-beginnings” story - or at least i hope i’m not, because i really did fight tooth and nail for this opportunity. i wouldn’t have died for it in p5, hell, i thought it was a lost cause and ruled it out as an option. but when i saw it creeping back to me in y1 i thought _why the hell not?_ now i didn’t really think i’d get anywhere far, i just thought i owed it to myself to try again. i picked it up as some academic pasttime, kind of like an extracurricular except i took it slightly more seriously than that. but extracurricular in the sense that you never really expect it to go anywhere. it’s great when you achieve in a classroom, surrounded by people whose capabilities aren’t out of the world, but once you step out there it’s a whole new terrain. you pick up some robotics module - _yeah no i’m not doing that after high school_. it’s all a high school thing, isn’t it? 

but i’m starting to see so much for this. i can see how it extends its way into university - where still, i will meet thousands of other brilliant people who snatched at such opportunities from a young age. they will be people who knew all along, that they were the 0.1% to stick to this path even after high school, even into university and the confusing world beyond. their life was settled for them, and they didn’t retreat into the same painful hole of doomscrolling, having a mid high school crisis, procrastinating, realising that you’re just going to have to be content with being in the top in your school. look any further and it’s no man’s land.

but now! now that i’ve wriggled my way into that top, elite circle of people. now that i’ve met people i admired from a distance, become fast friends…now that i can see such a life for myself. it’s amazing to see how quickly life can turn in your favour. it’s also an excruciating process, you’re always at the most precarious position but now that i’ve fallen and crawled my way back up, i hope i never have to look back.
