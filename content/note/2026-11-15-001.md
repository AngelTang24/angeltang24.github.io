Usually I judge writers first by their most accessible prose, which is the _How to Write_ or _Why I Write_ essay lurking somewhere at the bottom of their archives. There I decide if their prose is of merit. I generally trust those who are good with words, because then I don’t believe that you can be particularly shit at anything. And it’s kind of like debates: they’re not intellectually honest, but the arguments are intellectually and rhetorically interesting. If you can woo me with your words, I will put you and your work on a pedestal and consume it in place of my morning Substack doomscroll.
