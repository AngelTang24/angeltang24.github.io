Got a reply from a debate coach I was seeking out today. I’m very optimistic now, because I feel my current growth isn’t stunted by any innate skill—in fact I think based off my current interpretation of debate I think I’m doing pretty stellar. But clearly my interpretation differs from that of the actual circuit, and that probably affects me a lot as a third speaker. (After all the third speaker no longer pushes out new content—which I think I can do reasonably—rather evaluates the debate based off some rubric.) It’s frustrating, because I’m pretty sure I’m not far off—otherwise the examples I’m learning from would sound way more different—but I’m also sufficiently far off for my speaker scores to suffer. Before you tell me there is no fixed job scope for a third speaker, and strategies can vary, I get that. But there surely must be some fundamental underlying principle backing those decisions. I think I’ve got that wrong.

And even if, it doesn’t work out for my speaker scores, I’m glad to have a coach adjudicate my debates. Else it’s the blind leading the blind—and that’s how it’s been for a while. You might as well have a heated conversation instead, that would probably be more gratifying than running 6 back to back speeches that abruptly end with no sort of satisfying conclusion.

Really hoping this works out.
