Yesterday I watched the S3 _Disneyland_ episode of Modern Family. It’s my favourite so far (I have another 8-ish seasons to work through). For one it does its “heartwarming family moment” well. I think the show tries to incorporate one of these every few episodes, and all of them feel genuine but this one most of all. I like Jay, who pulls off a kind of sarcastic attitude well but also deeply cares for his family. In the episode he matter-of-factly reveals that he stuck through an unhappy marriage to watch his kids grow up. The episode ends with him and his now grown children (Claire and Mitchell) revisiting the Lincoln exhibit at Disneyland--the exhibit that had, on the night he contemplated divorce, convinced him that there were things worth staying for.

I also sympathise with his recount of the argument with Dede. He sounds the slightest bit shaken still, though it’s probably been decades. 

> Jay Pritchett: I don't know what happened. Maybe it's what Robot Lincoln said about a man's duty or keeping the union together. Maybe I just chickened out. But I realized that staying with my kids was more important than leaving my wife. Now, that's not the right decision for everyone, but it was the right decision for me. So I stuck it out until they were grown.  
>  
> Gloria Delgado-Pritchett: Jay, you want to join me in the Jacuzzi?
>  
>  Jay Pritchett: And the universe rewarded me.

That was really all it took for me to consider this episode my current favourite.
