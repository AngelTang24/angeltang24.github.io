> it will pass but it will also linger

Finally one of those quick takes that actually make sense and don't just sound sentimental for the sake of it. Not to live in the past, but my mind reroutes its way to a memory by the least expected links. I'm recovering better from each instance, but it's hard to entirely shake off the feeling that settles in your gut. That's the lingering part I think, and I don't exactly hate it but it isn't the best feeling to resume work to. I don't know who I would be without those memories though.
