# 2026
## November 
The birth of this site. For the comprehensive process of deploying this site, refer here. 

19 Nov: Changed the font from EB Garamond to Georgia (24 Nov: later on to Fira Sans)
![19 Nov change](/images/19nov-before.png)
EB Garamond was a really nice font and I was sad to see it leave, but it didn't fit the page. This could be a personal preference, but Georgia was a great improvement. I guessed it could be the increased x-height. Update: It increased with Fira Sans as well. 

23 Nov: Moved the nav bar to the side, so the image could move to the top

24 Nov: Changed the header image to solid colour (instead of an ink sketch) to better fit the font chosen.
